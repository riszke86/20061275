About This Habitat

Guardians of the Savannah is inspired by the vast grasslands of Africa, where some of the world's most recognisable animals live together. Visitors can observe lions, elephants, giraffes, zebras and other remarkable species while learning about conservation, migration and the importance of protecting endangered wildlife.

Conservation Message

Savannah wildlife faces threats from habitat loss, climate change and illegal hunting. This habitat helps visitors understand why protecting migration routes, supporting conservation projects and reducing wildlife crime are essential for the future of African species.
