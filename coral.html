Coral Cove

Dive beneath the waves and discover colourful reefs, graceful sea creatures and extraordinary marine life.

About This Habitat

Coral Cove recreates the beauty and biodiversity of tropical coral reefs. Visitors can explore colourful fish, sharks, rays, sea turtles, seahorses and other marine species while learning how coral ecosystems support ocean life and why protecting the world's seas is essential.

Conservation Message

Coral reefs support thousands of marine species but are threatened by warming oceans, pollution, overfishing and plastic waste. This habitat teaches visitors how responsible choices, cleaner oceans and marine conservation projects can help protect life beneath the waves.
