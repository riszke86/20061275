About This Habitat

Reptile Realm introduces visitors to remarkable reptiles from tropical forests, wetlands, deserts and river systems. Discover crocodiles, snakes, lizards, tortoises and other cold-blooded animals while learning how they regulate body temperature, hunt, defend themselves and survive in challenging habitats.

Conservation Message

Reptiles are essential to healthy ecosystems, but many species are threatened by habitat destruction, pollution and illegal wildlife trade. This habitat teaches visitors why protecting wetlands, forests and breeding populations is vital for reptile conservation.
